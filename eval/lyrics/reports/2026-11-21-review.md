## Reading

- **Aligners have hit the gold's noise floor.** Three independent aligners —
  mtl 32.0 %, w2v-bert 32.0 %, sylla 31.8 % — land within 0.2 pts on the same
  audio and text, and w2v-bert's line starts sit within 100 ms of mtl's on
  87.4 % of 1123 lines (only 7 identical). When different models agree that
  closely and still match only a third of the gold lines within 400 ms, the
  limit is the gold's own line timing (LRCLIB / Spotify / YouTube, #129), not
  the aligner. This metric can no longer separate aligners; a hand-verified
  gold subset is the next step before any aligner swap is argued on quality.
- **Speed is the only real aligner difference:** w2v-bert / sylla align a
  song in ~12 s on dev1's 2070 vs mtl's 65 s (98.5 s on win-resolume's
  3070 Ti, where mtl also needed the CUDA-OOM → CPU fallback on the longest
  song). Sylla wins clean_pop / instrumental_breaks by 6–9 pts but loses
  multi_language by 9 — category samples are 2–4 fixtures each, too small
  to act on.
- **No-text tier: no clear winner.** w2v-bert on the full mix reads 21.9 % vs
  g35t 21.3 % on the headline, but 12.2 % vs 15.6 % on the order-respecting
  view, and it over-produces lines 1.6× (greedy CTC words split choruses into
  many short lines that bind to the nearest repeat). g35t stays.
- **HeartTranscriptor is unusable for either tier:** Whisper DTW word times
  are back-to-back (median gap 0 ms, not one gap ≥ 400 ms in 6460 words), so
  no line structure can come from them, and with language auto it emitted 74
  CJK words on English songs.
- **The vendors' numbers are not ours.** Every claim above is word error on
  read speech (FLEURS, Common Voice) or word onsets on JamendoLyrics with the
  vendor's own aligner. Ours is line onsets of live worship material vs
  third-party line gold. Sylla's 95.9 % < 0.3 s and our 31.8 % ≤ 0.4 s are
  measuring different things; neither contradicts the other.
