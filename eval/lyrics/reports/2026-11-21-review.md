## Reading

- **The aligners tie.** mtl 32.0 %, w2v-bert 32.3 % and sylla 31.7 % on the
  same audio and text — within 0.6 pts, under the 0.8-pt move the mtl
  anchor itself showed between two copies of the same audio. These are two
  model families (sylla is a fine-tune of w2v-bert). mtl and w2v-bert place
  86.9 % of the 1123 line starts within 100 ms of each other; sylla places
  only 17.3 % within 100 ms of mtl's, yet matches the same share of gold.
  Our hypothesis: the gold's own line timing (LRCLIB / Spotify / YouTube,
  #129) now caps this metric, so it cannot separate aligners. Only a
  hand-verified gold subset (#129) can confirm or refute that; until then no
  aligner swap can be argued on quality.
- **Speed is the measurable aligner difference:** w2v-bert / sylla align a
  song in ~11–12 s mean on dev1's 2070 vs mtl's 65.5 s (5.4–5.8×; 98.5 s mean on
  win-resolume's 3070 Ti, where the longest song also fell back to CPU at
  585 s). Per-category differences (sylla +5 on clean_pop and +6 on
  instrumental_breaks, −9 on multi_language) stand on 2–4 songs each — too
  few to act on.
- **No-text tier: no clear winner.** w2v-bert reads 23.0 % (vocal or mix) vs
  g35t 21.3 % on the headline (+1.7), but 13.2 % vs 15.6 % on the
  order-respecting monotonic view (−2.4), 41.8 % vs 42.9 % conditional, and
  it over-produces lines 1.7× (greedy CTC words split choruses into short
  lines that bind to the nearest repeat). On the vocal it wins 5 of 6
  categories on the headline and loses instrumental_breaks (25.7 vs 32.1).
  The headline gap is also of the size the audio source alone moved mtl
  (+0.8), and g35t could not be re-run on the dev1 audio. Not
  a clear margin; g35t stays. Worth the same-audio re-check below.
- **HeartTranscriptor is unusable for either tier:** Whisper DTW word times
  are back-to-back (median gap 0 ms between consecutive words), so the 400 ms
  grouping makes 133 lines over 19 songs (~0.1× the gold's lines), and with
  language auto it emitted 74 CJK words on English songs.
- **The vendors' numbers are not ours.** Every claim above is word error on
  read speech (FLEURS, Common Voice) or word onsets on JamendoLyrics with the
  vendor's own aligner. Ours is line onsets of live worship material vs
  third-party line gold. Sylla's 95.9 % < 0.3 s and our 31.7 % ≤ 0.4 s
  measure different things; neither contradicts the other.
